Human-like Patterns

Variable keystroke timing, natural bursts, mid-word hesitation — just like a real person typing.

Realistic Mistakes

Occasional typos with backspace corrections. Optional “leave mistakes” mode skips the backspace.

⏰

5-Second Cooldown

Click “Type It”, switch to your target window, and the text flows in naturally after a short delay.

🎨

Skill Modes

5 profiles — Beginner, Average, Expert, Pro, and Bot — each with distinct speed, error rate, and rhythm.

🔒

Anti-Detection

Variable key hold duration, speed warmup, drift, and jitter make automated detection extremely difficult.

⌨

No Console

The .pyw extension + built-in window-hiding means zero terminal clutter. Just the GUI.
